    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. Stay tuned on LinkedIn, Instagram or the group chats for events.

    Do I need coding experience?

    No. Any degree. Learn to code and use AI tools.

    Do I need a team already?

    No, not at all.

    Not at Western, can I come?

    If it's a virtual event, yes, if you're in the broader network. You can also apply to be an ambassador and join the network.

    Can I start a chapter at my school?

    Yes. Email devnetwork8@gmail.com with a bit about you and your school.

    What happens at events?

    Workshops, demos and build sessions with AI tools, plus hackathons.
