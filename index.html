Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Anyone can join.

Do I need coding experience?

No. Anyone can join, learn how to code, and use AI tools.

Do I need a team or product already?

No, not at all. Come and learn.

I'm not at Western, can I come?

Yes for virtual events, or if you're in the London area, just submit a response and show up. You can also apply to be an ambassador and join the network.

Can I start a chapter at my school?

Yes. Email devnetwork8@gmail.com.

What happens at events?

Workshops, demos, build sessions with AI tools, and hackathons. We talk through ideas and what we're building.
